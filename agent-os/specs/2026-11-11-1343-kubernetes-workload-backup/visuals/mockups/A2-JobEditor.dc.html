How volumes are backed up: each volume gets a CSI snapshot for the few minutes its data is copied, then the snapshot is deleted. A volume is crash-consistent on its own; pre/post hooks on the pods (Velero's annotation names) flush or lock applications first. vSphere CSI takes the volumes of a namespace one after another, not at one moment.
